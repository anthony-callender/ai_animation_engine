# Brief
- Audience: Someone learning to turn a meeting note into an action card.
- One outcome: Turn one sentence from a meeting note into an action card with three fields (action, owner, due day), then check the card against the note.
- Sources: One fictional note, saved word for word in 01_voice/source.md: "Sam will send the draft on Friday." There are no other sources.
- What the viewer can do:
  1. Find the action: "Send the draft".
  2. Find the owner: "Sam".
  3. Find the due day: "Friday". Keep it as a day, not a calendar date.
  4. Compare the finished card with the original note. Every field should point back to words in the note, and the card should add nothing.
- Captures: None. No real app, calendar or task tool is shown.
- Authored examples: The note and the action card are authored illustrations and are labelled fictional on screen. The card has exactly three fields: Action: Send the draft / Owner: Sam / Due: Friday. It has no date, recipient, time, priority, follow-up or extra tasks.
- Wording note: "will send the draft" in the note becomes "Send the draft" on the card. Only the tense and capital letter change; no words are added.
- Format: Wide 16:9 with the pixel style (project defaults). About 30 seconds. One scene with four beats: the note appears (about 5s), the three fields are pulled out one at a time (about 15s), the card is checked against the note (about 7s), then a short recap (about 3s).
