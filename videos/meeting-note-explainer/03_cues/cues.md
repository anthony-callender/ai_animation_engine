## note "Here is a fictional meeting"
A paper note card slides in at centre. Chip: FICTIONAL NOTE · AUTHORED ILLUSTRATION.

## note.read "Sam will send the draft"
The note's one line types on as it is spoken: "Sam will send the draft on Friday."

## card "Let's turn it into an"
The note moves to the left half. An empty ACTION CARD appears on the right with three blank rows: Action / Owner / Due.

## action "First, the action"
The Action row lights in amber. The other two rows dim.

## action.find "What needs to happen"
An amber underline sweeps under "send the draft" in the note.

## action.fill "Send the draft"
The underlined words lift off the note and drop into the Action row as "Send the draft". A small check mark appears.

## owner "Next, the owner"
The Owner row lights in amber.

## owner.find "Who will do it"
An amber underline appears under "Sam" in the note.

## owner.fill "Sam"
"Sam" drops into the Owner row. Check mark.

## due "Then, the due day"
The Due row lights in amber.

## due.find "When is it due"
An amber underline appears under "Friday" in the note.

## due.fill "Friday"
"Friday" drops into the Due row as plain text, with no date and no calendar. Check mark.

## check "Now compare the card with"
Side by side: the note on the left and the finished card on the right, the same size and level. Chip: CHECK AGAINST THE SOURCE.

## check.action "Send the draft"
A line draws from the card's Action row to "send the draft" in the note. Both glow.

## check.owner "Sam"
A line draws from Owner to "Sam".

## check.due "Friday" +0.6
A line draws from Due to "Friday". The +0.6 offset is there because Whisper timed this "Friday" at 24.30, but the audio has it at about 24.9 (REVIEW.md).

## check.source "Each field comes straight from"
All three lines glow together. Every word on the card is now linked to the note.

## check.nothing "Nothing was added"
A plate under the card reads: Nothing added. The card still has exactly three rows.

## check.end "No calendar date, and no"
The plate adds a line: No calendar date. No extra work. Hold the side-by-side to the end (33.7s).
