# Narration draft (Reel A)

Headline on screen: HOW MY AI CHURCH WEBSITE WELCOMES NEW FAMILIES. One paragraph per step.
```
At 9 PM, a mom lands on a church website for the first time and asks, what time is service, and is there anything for my kids?

In seconds, my church assistant answers from the church's own approved info, then asks if she'd like to plan a visit.

My plan-a-visit agent saves her family's details and texts her a confirmation for the 10 AM service.

Saturday evening, my welcome agent texts her where to park, where kids check in, and a short welcome video from the pastor.

My greeter agent puts her family into the church's people database and gives the welcome team a heads up, so someone is waiting at the door.

On Monday, my follow-up agent texts a thank you and asks how it went. If they loved it, it invites them to a newcomers lunch. If something went wrong, it tells the pastor first.

Meanwhile, my sermon agent turns Sunday's video into a transcript, notes and a website post, and an editor approves it.

My social agent turns the sermon into posts for Instagram and Facebook.

Then my strategy agent reads what visitors asked the assistant, finds questions the website never answers, and drafts an FAQ for each one. My GEO agent checks them against Google and ChatGPT guidelines before they go live.

So when the next family asks ChatGPT for a church near them with a great kids ministry, your church is ready to be the answer, and the whole flywheel starts again.

We built a system that turns your church website into this digital front door. If you lead a church, comment BUILD and I'll send it over.
```
