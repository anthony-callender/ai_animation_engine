# Narration (Reel: Ambition Isn't a Sin), idea I-001, approved by Tony 2026-10-20; millionaire line replaced 2026-10-21 (Tony: wealth can be a genuine calling), from voice note 12.10.16 PM

Mission reel, paper collage. Source: Godly Ambition package Reel 3. Aquinas: Summa Theologiae II-II q.129 (magnanimity), building on Aristotle.
```
Christian, being ambitious is not a sin.

There's a virtue called magnanimity. Aristotle wrote about it, and Saint Thomas Aquinas developed it within Christian thought.

It's about aspiring to great things, for the right reasons.

And brother, you can become excellent without becoming proud. Bold without becoming harsh. Successful without becoming corrupt.

Maybe you're called to fix roofs. Then become excellent at fixing roofs.

Maybe you're a baker. Make the best bread in your town.

Maybe you're a programmer. Build technology that genuinely helps people.

Jesus worked as a craftsman.

Our work matters.

We don't need everybody to become famous Christian influencers.

We need faithful Christians doing excellent work in ordinary places.

Whatever your calling is, take it seriously. And use it to serve others.

Send this to a Christian who needs to hear it.
```
