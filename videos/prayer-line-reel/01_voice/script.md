# Narration (LinkedIn #16: "Should AI handle prayer requests?"), from Tony's own comment replies (Charles thread), 2026-11-17

Follow-up to #14 (3 filters video, 663 impressions). Same dark UI kit. Tony's words, lightly adapted for voice.
```
Should AI handle prayer requests?

It depends on what you mean by handle.

Praying for someone? That's always a person. Never a machine.

But getting the request to the right person? A machine can help with that.

Here's where I draw the line.

When someone asks for prayer, they often share the hard part too. A diagnosis. A marriage. A son who stopped calling.

That shouldn't sit in a chatbot's history, or be sent to an outside AI.

So prayer requests get their own form, and it goes straight to the pastor.

And if someone types one into the chat anyway, a small local filter catches it before anything is sent out, and routes it privately to the pastor.

The AI never prays. It just gets the request to someone who will.
```
