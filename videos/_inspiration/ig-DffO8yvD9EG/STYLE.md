# Reference reel: "Most AI agents should be a workflow" (IG, @shanepduggan), saved 2026-11-09

Tony's chosen format for the LinkedIn videos in the week of 2026-11-09.

**Specs:** 720×1280, 30 fps, 102 s. Files here: `reel.mp4`, `sheet.png` (a frame every 2 s), `detail.png`, and the transcript (below).

## Structure
1. **Hook (0–3 s):** a contrarian claim. The title appears at the top only for the first few seconds.
2. **One small, concrete example carries the whole video:** "one small online shop that sells candles". Every idea is shown on that one business.
3. **Analogy:** a workflow is like a recipe; an agent is like a new staff member.
4. **The example plays out step by step,** one UI object at a time: an order card, a spreadsheet row, a thank-you email, a phone notification.
5. **The twist or risk, made visual with numbers:** "say 95 / 100", shown as a 10×10 dot grid with red misses, then 10 steps leading to 60%, shown as parcel boxes.
6. **A simple test the viewer can use:** "Could you write the steps on a sticky note?"
7. **The best answer is a hybrid:** the AI does one small job, then the workflow takes over.
8. **An authority close:** the Anthropic "Building effective agents" page, with the key line highlighted.
9. **CTA:** "Comment TRACK", then a cheat-sheet visual, then an avatar and Follow card. **On LinkedIn, we drop the comment-keyword CTA.**

## Look
- Near-black background (#0b0b0d) with a faint dot grid. No other decoration.
- **Accent: a glowing lavender orb (about #a78bfa)** that *is* the AI. It appears on a "STAFF" badge card and as the decision node.
- **UI objects:** dark cards with thin green (#22c55e) borders for "done", plus white email cards, phone lock screens, a cream recipe card and a yellow sticky note with handwriting.
- Only one or two objects on screen at a time, in the upper-middle area. They animate in with small pops, and checkmarks tick on.
- **Captions:** white bold sans (Inter or SF style), 3–5 words at a time, centered at about 62% of the height. No box behind them, and no word-by-word color highlight.
- The pacing is fast: a new visual beat every 1.5–3 s, and nothing static for longer than about 3 s.
