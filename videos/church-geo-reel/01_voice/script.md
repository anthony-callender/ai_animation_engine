Ask ChatGPT for a church near you with a great kids ministry. Is your church in the answer?

Everyone's selling GEO, generative engine optimization, like it's a secret trick. It isn't. Google literally says it's still SEO. Here's what actually works.

Step one. Make sure AI can find you. Your site has to be in Google, and it can't block OpenAI's search crawler, or ChatGPT search won't show it.

Step four. Post what only your church has. Your sermons, your stories, your events. Google says don't just recycle what everyone else says.

Step five. Earn real reviews and real mentions, like local news and community events. Never fake them. Google says fake mentions don't help.

And skip the hype. You don't need a special AI file for Google, and nobody can guarantee you'll show up in ChatGPT.

Then test it. Ask ChatGPT and Google the questions a new family would ask, and see if your church shows up.
