```
Can a church chatbot make things up? Honestly, yes. If it's built wrong, it'll guess your service times.

Here's how I build mine so it doesn't.

First, the church writes its facts once. Service times, kids ministry, parking, beliefs.

When a visitor asks a question, the assistant pulls only the approved facts that match.

Then it answers from those facts, and nothing else.

If the answer isn't there, it says it's not sure and points them to the church office.

And when the church updates a fact, the assistant updates too.

That's called grounding. The church's own words, instead of a guess.

Comment BUILD and I'll send you a free teardown of your church website.
```
